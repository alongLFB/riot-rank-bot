LegendScout Discord Bot - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-11

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data LegendScout ("the Bot") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data We Collect

To provide League of Legends stats tracking, the Bot may collect and process the following data:

  • Discord Data: User IDs, Server IDs, and Channel IDs to respond to commands and manage bot configuration.
  • Game Data: League of Legends Summoner Names, Riot IDs, and regional identifiers provided by users.
  • Public Game Stats: Match history, tier/rank information, and performance data fetched directly from official/public Riot Games APIs.

2. How We Use Data

We use the collected data strictly to:

  • Execute user commands (e.g., fetching and displaying your LOL stats).
  • Cache game data temporarily to reduce API load and improve response speed.
  • We NEVER sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Temporary game data caches are automatically cleared periodically.
  • If you wish to have your linked accounts or configuration data deleted from our database, you can use the `/unlink` command (if available) or contact the developer directly.

4. Third-Party Services

The Bot interacts with the Riot Games API and Discord API. Please refer to Riot Games’ Privacy Notice and Discord’s Privacy Policy for information regarding how they handle data.
